Welcome to Our Cricket Club

How to play cricket and rules to follow

Cricket is a strategic bat-and-ball game. Two teams compete to score the most runs while trying to get the opposition out.

  • Bowlers target the wickets
  • Batsmen score runs
  • 11 players per team

Our club schedule runs every Thursday and Friday at lunchtime. We have a full plan for skills, drills, and matches for the entire year.
